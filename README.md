Welcome to my portfolio! This guide will help you navigate the site and understand how to read my work.
 

What this portfolio is about

This portfolio showcases my projects, process, and growth as a designer/developer/creator. It’s not just a highlight reel. I focus on authenticity, learning, and how I’ve grown through each experience.


How to navigate
    •	Home: A quick overview and introduction.
    •	Projects: Dive into the work I’ve done. Each project includes   background, process, challenges, and outcomes.
    •	Learning outcomes: Explore the work I've done to show how I’ve met each learning outcome.


What to look for
    •	Process over perfection — I care about how things are made, not just how they look.
    •	Feedback and iteration — I include examples of how I responded to critiques and changed things.
    •	Personal touch — I’ve made design choices (like colors, layout, tone) to reflect who I am, not just to follow trends.
