Vision Installatietechniek

Vision Installatietechniek is a one-man business owned by Ali, a 51-year-old professional from Helmond. After years of working for various employers and doing work he didn't enjoy, Ali made a bold decision in 2022 to start his own company and pursue the kind of projects he's passionate about.
Since then, Ali has built a strong client base and completed a wide range of projects, earning a reputation for quality and reliability. However, with new regulations in the Netherlands requiring all sole proprietorships to have an online presence, Ali realized it was time to create a website for his business.
That's where I came in. Ali asked me to design a website that showcases some of the major projects he has completed, outlines the full range of services he offers, and includes a contact form so potential clients can easily reach out with their details and needs.

Research

I began this project by researching existing websites for electricians to understand common design patterns and content structures. Ali sent me a website he liked, and mentioned he wanted his site to have a similar look and feel. I quickly discovered that there aren’t many well-designed websites for electricians, so I expanded my research to include websites for other tradespeople, such as plumbers and general handymen.
Many of these websites were quite basic and lacked detailed information. In contrast, the websites of more professional, larger companies included more comprehensive content, which I found more engaging and informative. I decided to take inspiration from those and aimed for a more content-rich, trustworthy design.
Ali provided a list of the services he offers, but it was up to me to find detailed descriptions for each. To do this, I looked at how other electricians present their services online. I used some of this content as a reference, and with the help of AI, I rewrote it into original, clear descriptions tailored to Ali's business.

Design Process

I faced several challenges when coming up with a design for the website. Ali already had a logo, but there was no existing visual identity or house style to work with. I chose blue as the main color, as it aligns well with the electrical industry and conveys professionalism. To create contrast and draw attention to key elements like buttons, I used orange as an accent color.
Initially, I planned to list all the services on the home page, with pop-ups for more details when clicked. However, that approach didn’t feel right—it looked too playful and lacked the professional tone I was aiming for. That’s when I decided that each service deserved its own dedicated page with in-depth information.
I still considered adding a brief overview of all services on the home page, but I couldn’t find a layout that looked clean and effective. In the end, I kept the home page focused on general information, while the navigation menu leads users to detailed service pages. You can view all of the trial and errors in the Figma document.
